# loot_rebalance (CN baseline / English ROM)

New mod (2026-10-31). Changes what crates, wooden/iron chests and monsters drop. It is built on
the loot map in [`../LOOT_GENERATION.md`](../LOOT_GENERATION.md).

- Definition: `Patches/Definitions/Custom/loot_rebalance.json` (stage `runtime_verified`),
  generated by `Patches/loot_rebalance/build.py`, which checks every preimage.
- Ship: `Patch_Manager/patches/loot_rebalance.bps` (93,835 B, SHA-256 `8e52571e…`) and
  `Patch_Manager/patches/cn/loot_rebalance.bps` (CN-compatible).
- **Allocation:** `0x09020000..0x0903654F` (91,472 B; was zero; owner `loot_rebalance`). It holds
  184 new item lists. Other writes: 64 loot rows at `0x084F4954 + (row-1)*36` (36 B each) and 4
  instruction bytes at `0x0805CDC0`, `0x0805CDC6`, `0x0805CDCC` and `0x0805CDD2`.
- The original item lists are untouched; the patched rows just point at the new ones. Gold chests,
  the special chests and the fixed rows (keys, souls, Red Shoe…) are unchanged.

## What changes

All rates are per break or per kill. The same changes apply in hard mode (rows +50).

| Source | Nothing | Gold | Consumable | Weapon | Armour | Accessory | Paste | Unidentified (of gear) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crate / barrel, vanilla | 75.0 | 6.2 | 7.0 | 5.1 | 6.2 | 0.4 | – | 25 % |
| Crate / barrel, **mod** | **0** | **44.1** | **44.1** | 5.1 | 6.2 | 0.4 | – | 25 % |
| Wooden chest, vanilla | – | 13.7 | 0.4 | 28.1 | 51.6 | 3.1 | 3.1 | 41 % |
| Wooden chest, **mod** | – | **0** | **14.1** | 28.1 | 51.6 | 3.1 | 3.1 | **50 %** |
| Iron chest, vanilla | – | 13.7 | 0.4 | 28.1 | 51.6 | 3.1 | 3.1 | 71 % |
| Iron chest, **mod** | – | **0** | **14.1** | 28.1 | 51.6 | 3.1 | 3.1 | **100 %** |
| Monster generic drop, vanilla | – | 25.4 | 27.7 | 19.5 | 24.6 | 2.0 | 0.8 | 25 % |
| Monster generic drop, **mod** (the 1 in 4) | – | **0** | **97.3** | **0** | **0** | 2.0 | 0.8 | – |

Monsters, per kill in single player (normal and hard mode alike, before the monster's own slots):

| | Vanilla | Mod |
|---|---|---|
| Nothing | 93.75 % | **71.9 %** |
| Main drop table (above) | 6.25 % | **25 %** (Herb 8.0, Goddess Tear 5.3, Healing Drop 4.0, Ray of Hope 2.7, Healing Water 2.0, Antidote 0.7, Bomb 0.7, accessory 0.5, stat foods 0.13 each, paste 0.2) |
| Valuing Scroll, own share | – | **3.125 % (1/32)** |
| Angel Wing, Grenade | in the table | **never** |

- **Crates:** the "nothing" entry is gone. Weapons, armour and accessories keep their exact weights;
  the rest is split evenly between the row's gold entry and its consumables (scaled together, so
  Herb stays the most common). Def 16 crates use row 0 and stay empty, as before.
- **Wooden/iron chests:** the gold entry is removed. Its 13.7 % goes to the **real consumables**
  (Herb, Healing Drop/Water, Goddess Tear, Ray of Hope, Angel Wing, Antidote, the stat foods,
  Valuing Scroll, Grenade, Bomb). They are spread like the consumables of the same tier's monster
  row, which is the list chests had no access to before. The chest's novelty items (Face
  Balloon, animals, letters…) keep their vanilla 0.4 %. Example, tier 1: Herb 3.2 %, Valuing
  Scroll 2.5 %, Goddess Tear 2.1 %, Healing Drop 1.6 %.
- **Enchant chance** (an enchanted item is unidentified): wooden row value 40 → 49, iron 70 → 100.
  The check is `value >= (rand() & 0xFFFF) % 100`, so a value v gives (v+1) % (40 was really 41 %).
  Gear with built-in effects is never enchanted, so a few iron-chest items still come identified.
- **Monster drop chance:** the generic roll is `killer+0x7C + bonus > (rand() & 0xFF)` (the
  `adds r0,#imm` bytes in `0x0805CCC8`). The bonus is now 9/8 of four times vanilla: 1 player
  16 → 72, 2 players 21 → 94, 3 players 26 → 117, 4 players 32 → 144. In single player that is
  64/256 = **1 in 4** for the main table plus 8/256 = **1/32** for the Valuing Scroll.
- **Monster drop table:** gold, all equipment, Valuing Scroll, Angel Wing and Grenade are removed;
  their weight goes to the remaining consumables, scaled proportionally (Herb, Goddess Tear,
  Healing Drop/Water, Ray of Hope, Antidote, Bomb, the stat foods, novelty items). Accessories
  (2.0 %) and pastes (0.8 %) keep their share of the main table. The Valuing Scroll is then 1/9 of
  the list (weight 7282 of 65536), which with the 9/8 chance makes it a separate 1/32 without
  touching the other odds. With more players or the Luck "Drop+1" prefix (`killer+0x7C`, *candidate*)
  the scroll scales along with everything else.
- **Advanced (hard) mode** runs the same roll on row +50, which is patched the same way, so the
  rates are the same. In hard mode a monster whose level `+0x78` is 0 never drops (vanilla rule,
  unchanged; which monsters have level 0 is not mapped).
- **Not changed:** a monster's own drop slots (`monster+0x3A4`, e.g. the goblins' card and Goblin
  Axe, the Lightning Paste and card of the monster in `Easy_Kill.ss0`). They are rolled before the
  generic roll and come on top of the 1 in 4.

## Evidence

`runtime_verified` on the English ROM + mod (`Patches/loot_rebalance/build_out/ss2-en+loot_rebalance.gba`,
SHA-256 `06381d74…`), normal mode, single player, visible input only:

- `loot_verify.py` (the model now reads its tables from `--rom`) on `Crate.ss0`, `Wooden_Chest.ss0`
  and `Iron_Chest.ss0`, delays 0–24: **75/75** breaks match the model on the patched lists. The crate
  gave 11 gold, 8 consumables and 6 pieces of gear, and was never empty. All 22 iron-chest gear
  drops were unidentified.
- `Patches/loot_rebalance/test_monster.py` on `Easy_Kill.ss0` (a level-30 monster, row 30), delays 0–199:
  **200/200** kills match the model on both ROMs. Drops: vanilla 15 (8 generic, among them
  Copper Armor, Mage Robe and Studded Gloves), mod 24 (17 generic, all consumables: Goddess Tear,
  Herb, Grenade, Valuing Scroll…). The other 7 in both are the monster's own slots. The delays are
  consecutive frames, so the counts are not independent samples; the 1-in-8 rate comes from the
  code and the byte, not from these counts. (Run on the first version, with the 1-in-8 bytes.)
- Second version (1 in 4 + 1/32 scroll): `test_monster.py` delays 0–199, **200/200** match; 57
  drops, 50 from the main table + scroll (5 Valuing Scrolls, the rest Herb, Goddess Tear, Healing
  Drop/Water, Power Wine, Quick Chicken, Ray of Hope, Bomb, 2 rings), 7 from the monster's own slots.
  No Angel Wing or Grenade. Same run with `--hard` (diagnostic write of bit 9 of `0x03003518`):
  200/200 match, the generator is called with level 80 instead of 30. Containers re-checked:
  75/75.
- Static: `build.py` asserts every row preimage, enchant value and bonus byte, and that the
  allocation is zero. Every new list sums to exactly 65536. `mod_conflicts.py`: no overlap with
  the translation or any other definition.

Not tested: a real hard-mode game (only the forced flag above), multiplayer bonuses,
and stacking with other mods. No other definition touches the loot rows or `0x0805CCC8`.

