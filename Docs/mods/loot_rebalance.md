# loot_rebalance (CN baseline / English ROM)

New mod (2026-10-31). Changes what crates, wooden/iron chests and monsters drop. It is built on
the loot map in [`../LOOT_GENERATION.md`](../LOOT_GENERATION.md).

- Definition: `Patches/Definitions/Custom/loot_rebalance.json` (stage `runtime_verified`),
  generated by `Tools/.venv/bin/python Patches/loot_rebalance/build.py` (assembles `loot.s`, checks
  every preimage, and writes the Patch Manager sidecar).
- Ship: `Patch_Manager/patches/loot_rebalance.bps` (139,454 B, SHA-256 `2f08b26f…`),
  `Patch_Manager/patches/cn/loot_rebalance.bps` (CN-compatible) and the options file
  `Patch_Manager/patches/loot_rebalance.json` (see "Patch Manager options").
- **Allocation:** `0x09060000..0x0909FFFF` (136,808 B used, to `0x09081667`; was zero; owner
  `loot_rebalance`; no definition or shipped patch writes there). It holds 344 new item lists.
  Until 2026-11-04 the lists were at `0x09020000..0x0903654F`; that range is free again.
  Second allocation `0x080BC1E0..0x080BC2FF` (248 B used; was zero, no references, no definition
  or shipped patch writes there; `0x080BC300` is enemy_hp_bars): `loot.s`. Other writes: 64 loot
  rows at `0x084F4954 + (row-1)*36` (36 B each), 4 instruction bytes at `0x0805CDC0`,
  `0x0805CDC6`, `0x0805CDCC` and `0x0805CDD2`, and three hooks: `0x0805CE08` (4 B),
  `0x0805CDD8` (10 B) and `0x0802810A` (4 B).
- The original item lists are untouched; the patched rows just point at the new ones. Gold chests,
  the special chests and the fixed rows (keys, souls, Red Shoe…) are unchanged.

## What changes

All rates are per break or per kill. The same changes apply in hard mode (rows +50).

| Source | Nothing | Gold | Consumable | Weapon | Armour | Accessory | Paste | Unidentified (of gear) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crate / barrel, vanilla | 75.0 | 6.2 | 7.0 | 5.1 | 6.2 | 0.4 | – | 25 % |
| Crate / barrel, **mod** | **0** | **44.1** | **44.1** | 5.1 | 6.2 | 0.4 | – | 25 % |
| Wooden chest, vanilla | – | 13.7 | 0.4 | 28.1 | 51.6 | 3.1 | 3.1 | 41 % |
| Wooden chest, **mod** | – | **0** | **14.1** (best HP 8.6, best SP 4.9, novelty 0.6) | 28.1 | 51.6 | 3.1 | 3.1 | **50 %** |
| Iron chest, vanilla | – | 13.7 | 0.4 | 28.1 | 51.6 | 3.1 | 3.1 | 71 % |
| Iron chest, **mod** | – | **0** | **14.1** (Angel Wing 13.5, novelty 0.6) | 28.1 | 51.6 | 3.1 | 3.1 | **100 %** |
| Monster generic drop, vanilla | – | 25.4 | 27.7 | 19.5 | 24.6 | 2.0 | 0.8 | 25 % |
| Monster generic drop, **mod** (the 1 in 4) | – | **0** | **97.3** | **0** | **0** | 2.0 | 0.8 | – |

Monsters, per kill in single player (normal and hard mode alike, before the monster's own slots),
with the default options:

| | Vanilla | Mod |
|---|---|---|
| Nothing | 93.75 % | **71.9 %** |
| Main drop table (above) | 6.25 % | **25 %** (healing 14.0, SP 8.0, Antidote 0.7, Bomb 0.7, accessory 0.5, stat foods 0.13 each, paste 0.2; which potion: see "Monster potions") |
| Valuing Scroll, own roll | – | **3.125 % (1/32)** |
| Angel Wing, Grenade | in the table | **never** |

- **Crates:** the "nothing" entry is gone. Weapons, armour and accessories keep their exact weights;
  the rest is split evenly between the row's gold entry and its consumables (scaled together, so
  Herb stays the most common). Def 16 crates use row 0 and stay empty, as before.
- **Wooden/iron chests:** the gold entry is removed. Its 13.7 % joins the chest's few real
  consumables (13.5 % in all). The chest's novelty items (Face Balloon, animals, letters…) keep
  their vanilla 0.6 %.
  - **Wooden chests:** only the **best HP and SP item for the map and mode**, split like the
    same tier's monster consumables (HP 8.6 %, SP 4.9 %; no Valuing Scroll, Angel Wing, Antidote,
    stat foods or bombs). Normal mode: Herb in dungeons 1–5 and 18, Healing Drop elsewhere; Goddess
    Tear. Advanced mode: Healing Water; Ray of Hope. The hard rows' lists hold Healing Water and
    Ray of Hope directly. The normal rows' lists hold Healing Drop and Goddess Tear, and `loot.s`
    `chest()` (the object break's `bl gen` at `0x0802810A`) turns a Healing Drop into a Herb when
    the row is a normal wooden row (a 64-bit row mask in the code) and the dungeon number is 1–5 or 18.
  - **Iron chests:** every consumable is an **Angel Wing** (13.5 %), normal and hard.
- **Enchant chance** (an enchanted item is unidentified): wooden row value 40 → 49, iron 70 → 100.
  The check is `value >= (rand() & 0xFFFF) % 100`, so a value v gives (v+1) % (40 was really 41 %).
  Gear with built-in effects is never enchanted, so a few iron-chest items still come identified.
- **Monster drop chance:** the generic roll is `killer+0x7C + bonus > (rand() & 0xFF)` (the
  `adds r0,#imm` bytes in `0x0805CCC8`). For "1 in N" the bonus is 256/N for one player, scaled
  per player count like vanilla (16/21/26/32): 1 in 4 is 64/84/104/128 (the default), 1 in 6
  43/56/69/85, 1 in 8 32/42/52/64, 1 in 10 26/34/42/51, 1 in 12 21/28/35/43. The Luck "Drop+1"
  prefix (`killer+0x7C`, *candidate*) adds on top, as in vanilla.
- **Monster drop table:** gold, all equipment, Valuing Scroll, Angel Wing and Grenade are removed;
  their weight goes to the remaining consumables, scaled proportionally (Herb, Goddess Tear,
  Healing Drop/Water, Ray of Hope, Antidote, Bomb, the stat foods, novelty items). Accessories
  (2.0 %) and pastes (0.8 %) keep their share of the main table.
- **Valuing Scroll from monsters** (`loot.s` `roll()`): the 10 bytes at `0x0805CDD8`
  (`cmp r5, sb; bls; movs r4, #1; cmp r4, #0; beq 0x0805CE4E`) become `bl roll; cmp r0, #1;
  bhi 0x0805CD64; bne 0x0805CE4E`. `roll()` returns 1 for the generic drop exactly as before
  (chance > sb). Otherwise it draws one more `rand() & 0xFF` and makes
  `v = (sb - chance) * 256 + that`, which is uniform over the "nothing" share. If v is below the
  threshold (u32 at `0x080BC2CC`, out of 65536; 2048 = 1/32 by default), it writes a Valuing
  Scroll (type 1, code 12) into the zeroed record and returns 2. The caller then exits through
  `0x0805CD64` (`movs r0, #1`, the monster's own-slot exit), so the scroll drops. So the scroll is
  exactly threshold/65536 of all kills, taken only from "nothing", whatever the drop rate. It
  does not scale with players or Luck (until 2026-11-04 it was 1/9 of the list and did). Level-0
  monsters get none, as they never get a generic drop either. The extra RNG call happens on
  every kill without a generic drop.
- **Monster potions** (`loot.s`). The roll's `bl gen` at `0x0805CE08` calls `wrap()` at
  `0x080BC1E0`, which calls gen and then re-picks a consumable with code 0–4. The healing and SP
  totals above stay the same; only which potion it is changes, uniformly among the allowed ones:

  | | Healing (Herb 20 HP / Healing Drop 50 / Healing Water 200) | SP (Goddess Tear 30 SP / Ray of Hope 100) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Normal, dungeons 1–5 and 18 (Goblin Fort, Giant's Graveyard, Wizari's Palace, Fairy Spring, Robert's Pirate Ship, Grandma Colita's Well) | Herb | Goddess Tear |
  | Normal, any other map | Herb or Healing Drop, 1/2 each | Goddess Tear |
  | Advanced (hard), any map | Herb, Drop or Water, 1/3 each | Goddess Tear or Ray of Hope, 1/2 each |

  The map is the dungeon number `[0x0300329C]` (u16), read from the user's six map states:
  Goblin Fort 1, Giant's Graveyard 2, Wizari's Palace 3, Fairy Spring 4, Robert's Pirate Ship 5,
  Grandma Colita's Well 18 (`Easy_Kill.ss0` and `Iron_Chest.ss0` are 6). Hard mode is `0x0800C780`
  (bit 9 of `0x03003518`). The pick is `base + ((rand() & 0xFFFF) * n >> 16)`, one extra RNG call
  only when there is a choice. Amounts are the consumable records' `+0x17A` (kind at `+0x178`: 1 HP, 2 SP; `0x083DCEBC`, stride
  `0x184`). Crate potions are unchanged (wooden and iron chests: above), and so are monsters' own drop slots.
- **Advanced (hard) mode** runs the same roll on row +50, which is patched the same way, so the
  rates are the same. In hard mode a monster whose level `+0x78` is 0 never drops (vanilla rule,
  unchanged; which monsters have level 0 is not mapped).
- **Not changed:** a monster's own drop slots (`monster+0x3A4`, e.g. the goblins' card and Goblin
  Axe, the Lightning Paste and card of the monster in `Easy_Kill.ss0`). They are rolled before the
  generic roll and come on top of the 1 in 4.

## Patch Manager options

`Patch_Manager/patches/loot_rebalance.json` (written by `build.py`) gives the patch the name
"Loot rebalance" and two drop-downs (format: `Patch_Manager/README.md`):

| Option | Choices | Writes |
|---|---|---|
| Monster drop rate | 1 in 4 (default), 6, 8, 10, 12 | the 4 bonus bytes `0x0805CDC0/C6/CC/D2` |
| Valuing Scroll from monsters | 1 in 32 (default), 30, 28 … 16 | u32 `0x080BC2CC` = round(65536 / N) (2048 … 4096) |

The `.bps` carries the defaults, so it is the same ROM without the sidecar or in the web patcher
(which ignores sidecars for now). The two options are independent: neither changes any list. On
the Chinese game the same JSON applies to `cn/loot_rebalance.bps` (same addresses).

## Evidence

`runtime_verified` on the English ROM + mod (`Patches/loot_rebalance/build_out/ss2-en+loot_rebalance.gba`,
SHA-256 `06381d74…`), normal mode, single player, visible input only:

- `loot_verify.py` (the model now reads its tables from `--rom`) on `Crate.ss0`, `Wooden_Chest.ss0`
  and `Iron_Chest.ss0`, delays 0–24: **75/75** breaks match the model on the patched lists. The crate
  gave 11 gold, 8 consumables and 6 pieces of gear, and was never empty. All 22 iron-chest gear
  drops were unidentified.
- `Patches/loot_rebalance/test_monster.py` on `Easy_Kill.ss0` (a level-30 monster, row 30), delays 0–199:
  **200/200** kills match the model on both ROMs. Drops: vanilla 15 (8 generic, among them
  Copper Armor, Mage Robe and Studded Gloves), mod 24 (17 generic, all consumables: Goddess Tear,
  Herb, Grenade, Valuing Scroll…). The other 7 in both are the monster's own slots. The delays are
  consecutive frames, so the counts are not independent samples; the 1-in-8 rate comes from the
  code and the byte, not from these counts. (Run on the first version, with the 1-in-8 bytes.)
- Second version (1 in 4 + 1/32 scroll): `test_monster.py` delays 0–199, **200/200** match; 57
  drops, 50 from the main table + scroll (5 Valuing Scrolls, the rest Herb, Goddess Tear, Healing
  Drop/Water, Power Wine, Quick Chicken, Ray of Hope, Bomb, 2 rings), 7 from the monster's own slots.
  No Angel Wing or Grenade. Same run with `--hard` (diagnostic write of bit 9 of `0x03003518`):
  200/200 match, the generator is called with level 80 instead of 30. Containers re-checked:
  75/75.
- Static: `build.py` asserts every row preimage, enchant value and bonus byte, and that the
  allocation is zero. Every new list sums to exactly 65536. `mod_conflicts.py`: no overlap with
  the translation or any other definition.

- Third version (potion re-pick): `test_monster.py` delays 0–199 on English+mod (normal and
  `--hard`) and English, **600/600** kill rolls match the model including the re-pick's RNG
  (`loot_model.monster_drop(post=…)`). Normal mode (dungeon 6) dropped only Herb, Healing Drop and
  Goddess Tear; hard mode all five. `Patches/loot_rebalance/test_potions.py` (diagnostic: forces
  the drop roll with r9 = 0 at `0x0805CDD8`, overwrites the dungeon number, the hard bit and the
  record right after gen with codes 0–4): 60 kills per case for dungeons 1–5, 18, 6, 7, 17, 19 and
  hard on dungeons 1 and 6, **720/720** results allowed. The six map states were only read for
  their dungeon number, not played.

- Fourth version (2026-11-04: options, separate scroll roll, wooden/iron chest consumables;
  `build_out/ss2-en+loot_rebalance.gba`, SHA-256 `11e6cd5c…`):
  - `test_monster.py` delays 0–199 (the model now includes the scroll roll, reading the
    threshold's address from the sidecar): default ROM normal and `--hard` **200/200** each, 52
    drops of which 4 Valuing Scrolls; the option ROMs built by `patch_manager.build()` from the
    JP ROM with "1 in 12" + "1 in 16" (bytes 21/28/35/43, threshold 4096) and "1 in 8" + "1 in 24"
    (32/42/52/64, 2731) **200/200** each; English **100/100**.
  - `Patches/loot_rebalance/test_chests.py` (compares after the object break's `bl` returns, so
    `chest()` is included), delays 0–59: `Wooden_Chest.ss0` (dungeon 1) **60/60**, consumables
    only Herb and Goddess Tear; same with `--dungeon 6` (diagnostic) **60/60**, Healing Drop and
    Goddess Tear; with `--hard` (diagnostic) **60/60**, Healing Water and Ray of Hope;
    `Iron_Chest.ss0` **60/60**, consumables only Angel Wing; `Crate.ss0` delays 0–39 **40/40**.
  - `test_potions.py` 30 per case: **360/360** allowed (`wrap()` is unchanged).
  - Patch Manager: the JP ROM + translation + the `.bps` with no options, and with the default
    options picked, equals `make_bps.py`'s ROM byte for byte; the option ROMs differ only in the
    bonus bytes and the threshold; the Chinese base + `cn/` version + options writes the same bytes.
  - Model (`loot_model.monster_drop`, 200,000 random RNG states, level 30, no own slots): defaults
    generic 1/4.02, scroll 1/32.06; "1 in 12" + "1 in 16" generic 1/12.00, scroll 1/15.89.
  - No shipped `.bps`/`.ips` writes in `0x09060000..0x0909FFFF`, `0x080BC1E0..0x080BC2FF`,
    `0x0805CDD8..0x0805CDE1` or `0x0802810A..0x0802810D` (decoded against the English ROM).

Not tested: a real hard-mode game (only the forced flag above), multiplayer bonuses,
and stacking with other mods. No other definition touches the loot rows, `0x0805CCC8` or the
object break `0x0802810A`.
