How the numbers work
  • Recipe chance = (goodwill + 1) / 65,536. Combinations with no recipe, and Mithril/Orihalcon/Dark Matter recipes whose forge values add up to less than 100, always give a random item.
  • +X is two rolls added together. Base roll: points = material points + gold ÷ 10,000 + 1, and the roll widens by (points × goodwill) ÷ 262,144. Gold roll: gold + a random 0–199,999, which gives +1 from 99,999, +2 from 188,506, +3 from 243,823, +4 from 278,396, and nothing at 300,000 or more.
  • Forge value = shown value ÷ 10 (Mithril), 25 (Orihalcon), 20 (Dark Matter), 1 (Bone) or 2 (Scale). Grade comes from the sum of the three forge values: under 100 is grade 0, then 100, 300 and 500. Material points: forge value ÷20 (Mithril), ×8÷100 (Orihalcon), ÷10 (Dark Matter), ÷100 (Bone, Scale), but the game uses the divisor of one randomly picked slot's material for all three slots. Pick that slot above to see each case.
  • Goodwill change = the three material values + gold ÷ 10 − the result's value, then clamped to 0 – 65,535. It never resets.
  • The result is capped at +20. A few weapon types always come out +0.
